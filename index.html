Research principles

This section shows the kind of designer I’m trying to become: someone who can explain not just what I made, but why I made it, what I learned, and how the research shaped the outcome.

Start with the user problem

Lead each case study with context, constraints, and what was actually broken before jumping into polished screens.

Show method choice clearly

Explain why you used surveys, interviews, card sorting, or usability tests so the reader trusts your process.

Translate insight into decisions

Show the jump from research findings to information architecture, interaction changes, and measurable outcomes.

Keep it skim-friendly

Use strong headings, compact summaries, and simple cards so busy recruiters can understand value in under a minute.
